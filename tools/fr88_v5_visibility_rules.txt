FR88 OCR VISUAL COMPLETENESS — REQUIRED V5 OVERRIDE

READ THE SCANNED INK, NOT WHAT THE BIBLE TEXT OUGHT TO SAY.

Especially at an image or page edge, book/chapter and verse digits may be partly or entirely out of view. A meaningful biblical citation can have invisible missing verse numbers. DO NOT complete these using biblical knowledge, neighboring references, structural rules, or intuition.

When an OCR reference stops at the visible page/crop edge:
* raw_line = ONLY the glyphs actually visible. Stop immediately before the missing/unreadable part.
* visibility_status = "CUT_OFF".
* visibility_note = a short description of which part is missing and what edge hides it.
* typography_uncertain = true.
* Do not append a probable verse, digit, hyphen, punctuation mark or book.
* Even if a printed row ends after a chapter number, don't automatically invent a verse.
* In particular, "1 Co 15" is a valid VISIBLE PREFIX of an incomplete print. Do not generate either "1 Co 15 45" or "1 Co 15 2" when the verse glyphs are not visible. This is a GENERAL RULE, not a transcription of the current image or a memorized answer.
* The difference between a visible chapter-only citation and a cut-off chapter+verse citation cannot always be resolved from a single scan. If uncertain, use visibility_status="UNREADABLE" or "CUT_OFF", not false confidence.
* A trailing comma at an edge may indicate a continuation. Do not invent its content.

Do not classify merely unusual but fully visible numeric rows as CUT_OFF; bare references such as "10 6" remain complete if visible.

For EVERY item add:
  "visibility_status": "COMPLETE" | "CUT_OFF" | "UNREADABLE",
  "visibility_note": ""
Set COMPLETE only when all characters of that physical printed row are actually visible.
CUT_OFF means one or more characters are obscured beyond the physical page edge or image boundary.
UNREADABLE means the row is present but a glyph cannot be distinguished.

If the evidence is too weak to confidently read a digit, leave it out of raw_line and flag UNREADABLE instead of guessing. This applies even when the guessed reference would be catalog-valid. Source-verse anchors remain null/unverified.

Preserve all other previous rules, including exact separate printed reference rows and the distinction between chapter bold type and ordinary verse type.
